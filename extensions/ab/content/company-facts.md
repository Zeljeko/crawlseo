## How AB works (Source: blog posts "3 Common Outsourcing Challenges", "Why Outsource to the Philippines", founder article)

- Starts by understanding the client's work, then puts the right people together with better processes to solve a specific business problem.
- Matches clients with professionals fluent in the client's preferred language.
- Clear communication, defined escalation paths and communication protocols.
- Quality assurance through feedback mechanisms and performance audits against agreed KPIs.
- Transparent pricing: detailed pricing structures and clearly outlined expenses in every contract. Flexible, scalable arrangements.
- Combines automation with human judgment for complex situations.
